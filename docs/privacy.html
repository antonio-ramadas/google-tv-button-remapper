Button Remapper privacy policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Button Remapper is provided by António Ramadas. Privacy questions: buttonremapper@gmail.com.

What the app accesses

With your permission, Android's accessibility service delivers remote button events to Button Remapper. The app processes these events locally to learn a button and execute the remaps you choose while other apps are open. It does not read screen content, record typing, or keep a button-press history.

The app reads the names and identifiers of launchable installed apps locally so you can choose which app a remap opens. It also checks whether supported TV settings and screensaver activities are available.

What stays on your TV

Button identifiers, names you assign, selected actions and target app identifiers, your consent choice, and the pause setting are stored in private app storage on your TV. This configuration persists across normal app and TV restarts. Android backups are disabled.

No off-device collection or sharing

Button Remapper has no network permission, accounts, advertising, analytics, or third-party SDKs. It does not transmit your configuration, button events, installed-app information, or other personal data to the developer or third parties. Apps opened by a remap operate independently and have their own privacy practices.

Retention, deletion, and control

Saved configuration remains until you remove it. Remove an individual remap to delete that remap. Choose Reset Button Remapper to delete all saved configuration and disable the accessibility service. Clearing app data or uninstalling also deletes the stored configuration. Pausing remaps retains your configuration. You can revoke accessibility permission in the TV's settings at any time.

Support and this website

If you contact the developer, the information you voluntarily send is used to respond to your request. Do not include passwords or private information in public GitHub issues. GitHub hosts this website and may log visitors' IP addresses for security; see the GitHub privacy statement. This is separate from the offline Android app.

Changes

This page will be updated if the app's privacy practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.
